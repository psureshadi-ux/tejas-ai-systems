# Tejas AI Systems - Standard Operational Runbook

> **Target Audience**: Operations Director (Wife) & Technical Director (Husband).  
> **Purpose**: Daily, weekly, and emergency operational protocols to run Tejas AI Systems with minimal friction.

---

## 1. Daily Operations Checklist (Operations Director)

### Morning Routine (10:00 AM - 10:30 AM)
1. **Access Owner Command Centre**: Log into `https://your-domain.com/admin` (or `http://localhost:3000/admin`).
2. **Review Inbound Inquiries**: Check `/admin/crm` for new leads submitted overnight via the website or ROI calculator.
3. **Dispatch WhatsApp Demos**: Click the "WhatsApp Pitch" button to send personalized demo links to newly qualified doctors or builders.
4. **Inspect Exception Queue**: Check `/admin/exceptions`. If any message failure or webhook issue is flagged, notify the Technical Director.

### Follow-up Routine (03:00 PM - 03:30 PM)
1. **Review Appointment Requests**: Verify patient OPD consultation bookings or real estate site visits scheduled by the bots.
2. **Proposal Review**: Check drafted proposals generated by the system for leads in the `PROPOSAL_SENT` stage.

---

## 2. Technical Operations Checklist (Technical Director)

### Off-Hours Maintenance (Evenings / Weekends)
1. **Agent Prompt Tuning**: Review conversation transcripts with `outcome = human_escalated` to identify common unanswered questions and add them to tenant knowledge bases.
2. **Database Backups**: Verify Supabase daily point-in-time recovery (PITR) status.
3. **Provider Costs & Quota Monitoring**: Check OpenAI / Gemini token usage and Razorpay settlement status.

---

## 3. Emergency & Incident Response

### Scenario A: Razorpay Webhook Failure / Payment Delay
* **Action**: Check `/admin/exceptions`. Search by order ID. If the payment was captured in Razorpay dashboard but webhook timed out, click `[Manual Reconcile]` to activate the subscription without charging the client twice.

### Scenario B: AI Provider Latency / Outage
* **Action**: Toggle the global switch `EMERGENCY_RULE_FALLBACK = true` in `/admin/settings`. This directs bots to collect patient contact details and preferred time slots using deterministic forms until the AI gateway stabilizes.
