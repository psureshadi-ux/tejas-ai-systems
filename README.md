# Tejas AI Systems - Tirupati AI Command Center

> **Solution**: 24/7 AI Lead & Appointment Specialist for Healthcare & Real Estate in Tirupati, AP.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (For You & Your Wife)

### 1. Launch the Command Center
Double-click or run:
```bash
py server.py
```
Open **[http://localhost:5000](http://localhost:5000)** in any web browser on your computer or laptop.

---

## 🌟 What This System Does Automatically

1. **Lead Discovery (`lead_scraper.py`)**:
   - Discovers clinics, dental practices, eye hospitals, pediatricians, builders, and real estate developers across Tirupati (Prakasam Road, Korlagunta, AIR Bypass, Bhavani Nagar, Karakambadi Road, Renigunta Road, etc.).
   - Pre-loaded with **54 verified Tirupati leads**.

2. **Automated Prototype Generator (`prototype_generator.py`)**:
   - Generates a customized, interactive, high-converting AI Web Assistant for each doctor and builder.
   - Includes real appointment booking, FAQ answering, OPD timings, pricing qualification, and a **Live Doctor/Builder Test Suite**.
   - Simulates the exact WhatsApp notification delivered to the doctor's or sales team's phone!

3. **Outreach Copilot (`outreach_engine.py`)**:
   - Crafts personalized, respectful pitches tailored to Tirupati local business etiquette (combining English and Telugu-English context).
   - Generates direct **WhatsApp Click-to-Chat URLs** (`https://wa.me/...`).
   - Your wife simply clicks **"🚀 Open WhatsApp & Send"** on the dashboard—WhatsApp opens with the prospect's number and custom pitch pre-filled. No copying, no phone saving, zero risk of Meta bot-spam bans!

4. **Explainer Video Scripts (`explainer_kit.py`)**:
   - Includes 60-second video walkthrough scripts in both **English** and **Telugu / Tanglish** for every lead.

## 📁 System Architecture

```text
Tejas-AI-Systems/
├── config.py                 # Agency branding, pricing, and phone settings
├── database.py               # SQLite database & lead management
├── lead_scraper.py           # Tirupati automated business discovery
├── prototype_generator.py    # Instant interactive AI bot website builder
├── outreach_engine.py        # WhatsApp & LinkedIn personalized pitch engine
├── explainer_kit.py          # 60-second video walkthrough scripts (EN & TE)
├── generate_all.py           # Batch generator for all prototypes
├── server.py                 # Flask Command Center & local web server
└── static/
    └── prototypes/           # 54 customized AI bot web applications
```

1. **Morning (10:00 AM)**: Open `http://localhost:5000`.
